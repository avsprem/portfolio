Akila Premathilaka

Data Scientist | Machine Learning and AI Enthusiast | Researcher

I am Akila Premathilaka, a graduate from the Faculty of Science, University of Peradeniya, holding a B.Sc. (Hons) in Data Science. As a wheelchair user, my personal journey has taught me strong resilience, practical problem-solving, and independence. I am highly passionate about leveraging data-driven approaches to explore advanced areas in Data Science, Machine Learning, and Artificial Intelligence.

My research interests lie in Machine Learning and Deep Learning with a focus on Explainable AI (XAI). I am also deeply passionate about Time Series forecasting, Image Processing, and the practical applications of Large Language Models (LLMs).
